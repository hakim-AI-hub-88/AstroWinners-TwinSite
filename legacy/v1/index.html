            Validation against known analog sites

            Run a search to compare our ranking with sites NASA already studied.

            How we validate. We do not change the model and we add no data to the calculation. After each analysis, we look up where sites that space-agency teams already studied as Mars analogs fall in our ranking — NASA and NASA-funded teams in the Atacama, the Austrian Space Forum's MARS2013 Mars simulation in Merzouga. A site picked at random would sit at the 50th percentile; a higher percentile means our system independently points to places NASA already chose.

            How to read it. Each NASA site stands for a different kind of Martian terrain, so agreement depends on the target: Licancabur (cold, high, rugged volcanic crater) should match rugged targets such as Gale Crater; Salar de Pajonales (high salt-flat basin) should match lake-basin targets such as Jezero Crater. Yungay was chosen for its extreme dryness, which our Earth–Mars–Moon features do not compare: a low rank there marks a limit of the method, not an error. Sites just outside the study area use the closest ~11 km cell.

            Data: NASADEM, MODIS, NASA POWER (Earth) · MGS MOLA / TES, Mars Odyssey GRS (Mars) · LRO LOLA / Diviner / LROC (Moon). Harmonized at ~11 km.
